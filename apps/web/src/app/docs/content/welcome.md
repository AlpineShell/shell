# Welcome to Alpine Shell

Hello, and welcome.

Alpine Shell is a **desktop shell replacement for Windows**. If you have used Linux, think of it like a desktop environment: it swaps out the parts of the desktop you look at and touch every day (the bar at the top, the desktop itself, the system controls) while Windows keeps running underneath. Your apps, your files and your hardware all work exactly as before.

## What you get

Windows ships one desktop experience and mostly leaves you with it. Alpine Shell replaces that layer with something calmer and cleaner: a simple topbar holding the controls that actually matter (volume, brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth), a tidy desktop, and none of the noise you never asked for.

- **A better Windows.** Sensible defaults and a clean interface, with the controls where you expect them.
- **No activated Windows license needed.** Alpine Shell runs on top of Windows and does not depend on activation, so it looks and works the same whether or not Windows is activated.
- **Clean on purpose.** Nothing blinking for your attention, no clutter. Just your computer.

## Why we made it

The goal is simple: make your computer **yours**, usable and clean. A desktop should get out of your way and let you get things done, not compete with you for attention. Alpine Shell is our take on what that can feel like on Windows.

Ready to make it your own? Add your own widgets to the topbar over in [Building plugins](/docs/plugins/getting-started).
